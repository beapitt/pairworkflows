Step-by-step workflow

Turn Claude into a receipt scanner — 5 steps
1

Replace the category list with your own

The default categories in these prompts are generic. Swap them for the exact categories your accounting software uses — QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Wave, or whatever you're on. Include codes like 6200 – Travel if you use them.

2

State your country's deductibility rules

Add a line like I'm a sole trader in the UK — apply HMRC expense rules when flagging deductible items. Claude isn't a tax advisor, but specificity gets you more relevant flags. Always confirm edge cases with your accountant.

3

Add a client column if you bill back expenses

Instruct Claude to include a "Client / Project" column and mark billable expenses with the client name. This makes invoice reconciliation much faster.

4

Run the batch prompt weekly or monthly

Paste a stack of receipts at once rather than one at a time. Claude produces one clean table with a summary line — total processed, total amount, total flagged deductible.

5

Paste every output into one running spreadsheet

Claude doesn't store anything between sessions. Your running expense log lives in a spreadsheet — paste each batch's output into the next empty row and you'll have a full-year record by December.

Not a tax advisor: Claude is not a tax advisor. Use its deductibility flags as a starting point, not a final answer. Anything flagged "Check with accountant" should go to your accountant before you file.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Can Claude read photos of receipts?
If you're using Claude.ai Pro or the web app, you can upload a photo of a receipt directly. Claude will extract the vendor, date, amount, and category. On the free plan, type or paste the receipt details as text instead.
How accurate is Claude at reading handwritten receipts?
Claude handles printed receipts well. Handwritten receipts are hit-or-miss depending on legibility. For handwritten receipts, it's faster to type the key details — vendor, date, and amount — and let Claude handle the categorization.
What expense categories should I use?
Use the categories that match your tax return or accounting software. Common freelancer categories include Software & Subscriptions, Travel, Client Entertainment, Professional Development, Office & Equipment, and Marketing. Tell Claude your category list in the prompt and it will stick to it.
Can I use this to track expenses across the whole year?
Yes — run the prompt in batches. Process a month at a time, paste the output into a running spreadsheet, and you'll have a full-year log by December. Claude doesn't store anything between sessions, so the spreadsheet is your record.
Will Claude flag potential tax deductibles?
Yes, if you include that instruction in the prompt. Add a line asking Claude to flag items that are commonly deductible for freelancers in your country. Always verify with your accountant — Claude is not a tax advisor.
