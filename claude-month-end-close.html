Claude AI for Month-End Close

Month-end close is the same structure every cycle — reconciliations, variance reviews, and a set of standard commentary. Build the prompt once and Claude turns this month's numbers into the narrative your management pack needs, consistently, every month.

Choose your situation — copy the prompt: 
Act as a Finance Manager. Turn the following month-end close steps into a
clear, sequential checklist for the team, grouped by close day.

Our close process steps (in any order):
[List your close tasks — e.g. bank reconciliations, accruals, prepayments,
fixed asset rolls, intercompany eliminations, journal review]

Close timeline: [e.g. 5 working days]
Team members involved: [e.g. AP, AR, GL, Financial Controller]

Format: group tasks by close day (Day 1, Day 2, etc.), assign each task to
a role, and flag any task that depends on another task being completed
first with [DEPENDENCY].

Build this once with your real close steps and save it. Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

Act as a Financial Accountant. Write the reconciliation narrative for the
following balance sheet account based on this month's reconciliation
results.

Account: [e.g. Accrued Expenses / Intercompany Receivable]
Opening balance: [figure]
Closing balance: [figure]
Reconciling items: [List each item with amount and brief description]

Format: one paragraph explaining the movement from opening to closing
balance, then a bullet list of each reconciling item with a one-line
explanation. Audit-ready tone — clear and factual, no informal language.

Paste figures and account names only — never client names, employee names, or bank account numbers.

Act as a Finance Business Partner. Write the variance commentary section
of this month's management pack from the following data.

Period: [e.g. May 2026]
Variances (line item, budget, actual, variance):
[Paste your variance data — line by line]

House style notes: [e.g. "Always explain the driver, not just the
number", "Flag anything over 10% with [REVIEW]"]

Format: one short paragraph per material variance line, explaining the
driver. Flag any variance over 10% with [REVIEW]. Professional, concise
tone — suitable for the monthly board pack.

State the period and house style notes every time so Claude matches your management pack format exactly.

Step-by-step workflow

Run month-end close with Claude — 5 steps
1

Paste figures and account names only

Never include client names, employee names, or bank account numbers. Amounts and account names are enough for every task in this workflow.

2

State the period and house style notes

Always include the period (e.g. May 2026) and any house style rules so Claude's output matches your management pack format on the first try, not the third.

3

Build each prompt once, then reuse it unchanged

Once a close prompt produces the right format, save it exactly as is. The value compounds when the same prompt produces the same format every month — changing it monthly defeats the purpose.

4

Cross-check every figure before it goes in the pack

Claude drafts the narrative — it does not perform the reconciliation. Every number must be checked against your trial balance and source systems first.

5

Flag material variances for senior review

Set a threshold (e.g. 10%) and have Claude flag anything over it with [REVIEW]. Claude flags — a human decides what the variance actually means.

Important: Claude drafts the narrative — it does not perform the reconciliation or verify the numbers. Every figure must be checked against your trial balance and source systems before the management pack is finalised.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Why is month-end close particularly well-suited to a Claude workflow?
Because it's genuinely the same process every month — reconciliations, variance reviews, and a set of standard commentary. Build the prompt once with your preferred format, and every cycle you only need to change the numbers.
What should you do once a close prompt produces the right output format?
Save it and reuse it unchanged every month. The value compounds when the same prompt produces the same format every cycle — changing it monthly defeats the purpose.
What must never be pasted into Claude during month-end close?
Client names, employee names, or bank account numbers. Paste figures and account names only — that's enough for every close task, and month-end figures should go through your organisation's enterprise Claude account.
What is Claude's role in the reconciliation process?
Claude drafts the reconciliation narrative — it does not perform the reconciliation or verify the numbers. Every figure must be checked against your trial balance and source systems before the management pack is finalised.
What should happen to variances Claude flags as material?
They should go to senior review. Claude can flag anything over a threshold you set (e.g. 10%) with a marker like [REVIEW], but a human decides what the variance actually means before it goes into the board pack.
