Frequently asked questions

What are the limitations of NotebookLM audio overviews?
NotebookLM generates engaging podcast-style audio conversations from your sources, but you cannot currently edit the script, direct the pacing, or change the specific AI voices used by the hosts.
How does NotebookLM ensure the accuracy of training materials?
NotebookLM grounds its answers and generated assets strictly in the source documents you manually upload. It does not pull information or hallucinate features from the broader internet.
Is it safe to upload confidential data to a client notebook?
Never upload unredacted legal contracts, proprietary financial data, or sensitive PII into a shared notebook if external users have access, as anyone with access can query the underlying source documents.
How should I verify AI-generated onboarding guides?
Always click the generated inline citations to check the instructions and steps against your original source documentation before distributing the material to clients.
What asset types can NotebookLM generate from documentation?
NotebookLM can automatically generate structured study guides, audio overviews, frequently asked questions (FAQ) lists, and support interactive querying with inline citations.
