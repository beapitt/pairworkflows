Be honest about your deal stage

2

3

List stakeholders by role

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Why is Claude useful for drafting a Mutual Action Plan?
Claude excels at logical sequencing — it takes your scattered deal requirements and organizes them into a coherent timeline based on the dependencies you define, rather than just listing tasks in a random order. It also maps each step to a named owner.
Why must a Mutual Action Plan be collaborative rather than something I send unilaterally?
A MAP is useless if you write it alone and send it as a requirement. It won't work if you force your own agenda without the buyer's buy-in on the steps and dates — you must co-create it with your buyer and treat it as a shared document you both own.
What does Claude not know when it drafts my Mutual Action Plan?
Claude can't know the specific internal political deadlines your buyer has, like their quarterly board meetings. You need to manually adjust the dates after talking to the buyer directly.
What should I do if Claude's suggested sequence doesn't fit my deal?
Manually adjust the sequence and dates based on your actual deal reality. Claude organizes logical dependencies from what you give it, but you know the specific political and procedural details of this deal that it doesn't.
What format should I ask Claude to build the Mutual Action Plan in?
A table with columns for Milestone, Required Action, Owner, and Suggested Deadline. This structure is easy for both sides to scan, easy to update as the deal progresses, and easy to paste directly into an email or shared document.
