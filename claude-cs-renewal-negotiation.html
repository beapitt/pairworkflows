Step-by-step workflow

Prep a renewal defense with Claude — 5 steps
1

Pull 12 months of account history

Grab meeting notes, support tickets, and usage metrics from your dashboard. The more complete the history, the sharper the risk assessment.

2

Note any known risks yourself

Jot down what you already know — that outage in October, a recent leadership change at the account — so nothing gets missed.

3

Paste it all into Claude

Drop the history and your risk notes into the prompt window and ask for a structured brief with value highlights and expected objections.

4

Rehearse the objection scripts

Read the AI's counter-arguments out loud to build muscle memory. If it sounds stiff or robotic, rewrite it in your own voice before the call.

5

Enter the negotiation prepared

Get on the call with your defense points ready, and route any commercial concession through your internal approval channel before promising it.

No commercial authority: Claude generates preparation material, not authorization. Always get renewal pricing decisions and discounts approved through your official internal channels before offering them to the client.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What is the main reason to use Claude AI before a renewal call?
Claude reviews your account history and usage data to spot hidden risks and surface undeniable value proof points, so you walk into the negotiation with a structured defense brief instead of relying on gut feeling.
What is a clear limitation of Claude during renewal prep?
Claude can't see the client's actual internal budget cuts or financial freezes. You still have to uncover those economic realities yourself during the live conversation.
What must I do before offering a commercial concession that Claude suggested?
Get the pricing decision or discount approved through your official internal channels first. Claude generates preparation material, not commercial authority.
Why should I read Claude's objection scripts out loud before the call?
To build muscle memory and make sure the response actually sounds like you rather than a stiff, generic script. Rehearsing out loud catches phrasing that reads fine on screen but sounds robotic in a live conversation.
How does Claude identify renewal risk from account history?
It scans your usage data, support tickets, and meeting notes for patterns — flat or declining usage, a champion who left, an unresolved complaint, or a competitor mention — and maps each signal to a recommended negotiation strategy.
