Frequently asked questions

How do I remove trailing spaces in Excel?
Use =TRIM(A2) for ordinary ASCII spaces: it removes leading/trailing spaces and reduces repeated internal spaces to one. Imported data can also contain non-breaking spaces or other characters that TRIM does not remove; CLEAN removes certain non-printing ASCII characters, and stubborn character-160 spaces may need SUBSTITUTE.
Why are my numbers stored as text in Excel?
When data is imported from external systems like CRMs or accounting software, numbers are often formatted with text characters or invisible apostrophes. Excel may treat imported amounts as text, which can cause arithmetic or aggregation formulas to ignore or mishandle them. VALUE() converts text that Excel recognises as a number, but currency and decimal/thousands separators must match the locale or be normalised first.
How do I fix dates that won't sort in Excel?
If the cells are confirmed as text dates, DATEVALUE() can convert recognised date text to Excel serial dates. Its interpretation depends on system/Excel date settings, so verify ambiguous formats such as 03/04/2026 before copying the formula down, then apply a Date number format.
What's the difference between TRIM and CLEAN in Excel?
TRIM handles ordinary space characters. CLEAN removes the first 32 non-printing ASCII characters, but neither function removes every possible imported Unicode character. If TRIM/CLEAN does not fix the value, inspect the character and consider SUBSTITUTE — for example replacing CHAR(160) non-breaking spaces.
