Why AI Matters for Marketing

Marketing teams are expected to produce more content across more channels than ever before — while still leading strategy, managing campaigns and reporting on results. The volume of writing, briefing and analysis required every week leaves little time for the work that actually moves the needle.

Claude and Gemini are most valuable in Marketing before the content is written — building the strategy, structuring the brief, defining the messaging framework. Get that right and the content that follows is faster, more consistent and easier to brief to any team member or agency. The creative direction and brand judgement always stay with you.

FAQ

Are these courses suitable for UK and US marketing teams?
Yes. Every workflow is written for UK and US corporate marketing contexts, with a focus on brand consistency and fact-checking before publication.
Will AI-generated content sound generic or off-brand?
The courses teach you how to feed Claude and Gemini your brand voice and tone before drafting, and how to edit every output so it sounds like your brand — not a generic AI default.
Do I need a paid Claude or Gemini plan to follow these courses?
No. Every workflow in these courses works on the free tier of Claude and Gemini. A paid plan may give you higher usage limits, but it is not required to follow along.
