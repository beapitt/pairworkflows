Best Practices for Using AI in Marketing

AI output in marketing is only as good as the brief you give it. These practices keep the output on-brand and useful.

Always do
  • Provide brand voice guidelines or examples of existing content
  • Include target audience and channel in every prompt
  • Specify the tone — professional, conversational, urgent
  • Give Claude a proper content brief, not just a topic
Never do
  • Publish AI content without human review and brand check
  • Use AI output as final copy without editing
  • Skip the brief — vague prompts produce vague content
  • Rely on AI alone for final campaign strategy decisions

FAQ

Can Claude write in our brand voice?
Yes — if you give it examples of your existing content and describe the tone. The more examples you provide, the closer the output. Always review before publishing.
Will AI-generated content hurt our SEO?
Not if the content is useful, original, and human-reviewed. Google rewards quality. The SEO course shows how to use AI to improve content quality, not just speed.
Can Claude plan a full content calendar?
Claude can structure a framework and suggest topics based on your brief. You make the strategic decisions — Claude handles the structure and drafting.
Can Gemini help with Google Ads copy?
Yes. Gemini can generate multiple headline and description variations for testing. One course covers the workflow for different ad formats.
Can AI replace a copywriter?
No. AI removes the blank-page problem and speeds up first drafts. It cannot replace creative direction, brand judgment, or the human instinct that makes copy actually convert. It makes good copywriters faster.
Can Claude write email sequences for different audience segments?
Yes. Describe the segment, the goal of the sequence, and the tone. Claude drafts the full sequence. You review, adjust for brand, and test.
Is it safe to paste campaign data into Claude or Gemini?
Anonymise where possible — remove client names and confidential figures. For internal use within your organisation's enterprise AI account, check your data policy first.
Do these courses require a paid Claude or Gemini account?
No. All workflows work on the free tiers of both tools. Paid plans give higher usage limits but are not required to follow any course on this page.
