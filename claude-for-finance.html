Best Practices for Using AI in Finance

The quality of AI output in Finance depends entirely on what you give it. These practices protect accuracy and keep you in control.

Always include
  • Actual figures — not placeholders
  • Reporting period and comparison period
  • Target audience — board, FD, or operational
  • Known business drivers behind the numbers
  • The accounting standard if relevant (IFRS or US GAAP)
When not to rely only on AI
  • Final sign-off on any regulated document
  • Figures feeding statutory or external reporting
  • High-stakes forecasts where a wrong number causes material harm
  • Raw sensitive data — anonymise names and figures first
  • AI drafts — Finance signs off. Non-negotiable.

How to anonymise before pasting: replace company names with Company A, client names with Client 1, sensitive margin figures with representative numbers that maintain the same ratios. The AI produces the same quality output — without the data risk.

FAQ

Can I use AI for board reporting?
Yes — and most forward-thinking CFOs encourage it. AI produces faster, cleaner drafts. The key is simple: AI drafts structure and language, you verify every figure before it goes anywhere near a board pack.
Is it safe to paste financial data into Claude or Gemini?
Only after anonymising. Replace company names, client names, and sensitive figures with representative alternatives that maintain the same ratios. The courses show you exactly how to do this safely without losing analytical value.
Can Claude write variance commentary from actual numbers?
Yes. Provide budget, actual, and prior year figures along with known drivers. Claude produces structured commentary you can review, refine, and send. The first draft is usually 80% of the way there.
Can Gemini extract data from PDF invoices?
Yes. Gemini in Google Workspace can read PDF invoices and extract structured data into Google Sheets. One course covers the exact workflow, including how to handle common formatting variations from different suppliers.
Can AI help with audit preparation?
Yes — on the narrative and documentation side. Claude can structure audit prep checklists, draft risk narratives, and rewrite technical language for auditor audiences. It cannot access your systems or verify figures independently. Human review before submission is non-negotiable.
Do these workflows work with IFRS and US GAAP?
Yes. Specify the accounting standard in your prompt and the output will use appropriate terminology. The courses include examples for both UK and US Finance contexts.
Can Claude help with cash flow forecasting?
Claude can draft the written narrative and structure the commentary around your forecast figures. It cannot generate the forecast numbers themselves — that requires your data and judgment. The course shows exactly where AI adds value and where it doesn't.
Do these courses require a paid Claude or Gemini account?
No. All workflows work on the free tiers of both Claude and Gemini. Paid plans give higher usage limits and more powerful models, but are not required to follow any course on this page.
