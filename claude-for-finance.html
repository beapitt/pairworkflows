Why AI Matters for Finance

Finance professionals spend a large share of their time on data collection and formatting rather than analysis. AI helps reverse that ratio — less time on administration, more time on the insight that actually informs decisions.

Whether you work to IFRS or US GAAP, boards expect precision and speed in equal measure. Generic AI use without the right guardrails carries real risk in a Finance context — but a properly structured workflow turns Claude and Gemini into a genuine force multiplier for variance analysis, reporting and forecasting.

FAQ

Are these courses suitable for UK and US finance teams?
Yes. Every workflow is written with UK and US corporate standards in mind — including IFRS and US GAAP terminology, and language suitable for both UK and US board reporting conventions.
Are these prompts safe for financial data?
The courses teach you how to anonymise sensitive figures and counterparty names before pasting them into Claude or Gemini, and when to use your organisation's enterprise AI account for sensitive financial data.
Do I need a paid Claude or Gemini plan to follow these courses?
No. Every workflow in these courses works on the free tier of Claude and Gemini. A paid plan may give you higher usage limits, but it is not required to follow along.
