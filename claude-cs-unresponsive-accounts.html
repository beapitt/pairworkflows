Step-by-step workflow

Re-engage a stalled account with Claude — 5 steps
1

Define the account context

Write down who the buyer is, what their original goal was, and the specific moment where the deal stalled.

2

Identify your last touchpoints

Note exactly what you said in your last few attempts and how many weeks it has been since they replied.

3

Paste it all into Claude

Drop the context and touchpoint history into the prompt so Claude knows exactly what's already been tried.

4

Generate the multi-channel plan

Ask for a specific Day 1 / Day 4 / Day 7 sequence mixing email, LinkedIn, and phone — not another single-channel guess.

5

Execute and track the response

Put the plan into action across your channels and monitor how the prospect reacts through your CRM or sales software.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Why is using Claude AI better than sending another standard follow-up email?
Claude forces you to pivot your angle and suggests multi-channel tactics instead of just repeating the same check-in message a fourth time. It looks at what you've already tried and generates a genuinely different approach.
What is a clear limitation of using Claude AI for re-engagement?
Claude can't track when a prospect actually opens your email or views your LinkedIn profile. You still have to monitor those engagement signals yourself through your CRM or sales software.
What should I do before pasting account information into Claude for this workflow?
Define the account context and identify your last touchpoints, including exactly how long it has been since the account went silent. Specific inputs produce a specific re-engagement plan.
What is the most critical rule when executing a re-engagement strategy?
You must respect opt-out preferences and comply with regulations like GDPR. Re-engagement must never cross into harassment — if a client tells you to stop contacting them, walk away immediately.
What does a multi-channel re-engagement sequence usually look like?
A typical sequence spreads tactics across a week: a value-led email and a LinkedIn touch on day 1, a short voicemail and follow-up email on day 4, and either a break-up email or a senior sponsor escalation on day 7 if there's still no reply.
