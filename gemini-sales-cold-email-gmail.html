Section 01

Writing personalized cold emails takes time. Gemini accelerates the drafting process directly within Gmail.

Crafting highly personalized cold emails that actually capture a prospect's attention is a time-consuming challenge for sales professionals[cite: 10]. Attempting to balance volume with meaningful personalization often leads to generic messaging that fails to convert or requires hours of manual writing[cite: 10].

Gemini helps solve this workflow bottleneck directly within your inbox using its integrated drafting capabilities[cite: 10]. By providing brief context about your prospect and offering, you can use the AI to instantly generate structured, customized email drafts that sound natural and professional[cite: 10].

Key insight: Gemini does not automatically know your prospect's background or company data — you must manually provide the context, pain points and specific value proposition to generate a tailored message[cite: 10].
Initial outreach drafts
Generate first-touch emails based on a prospect's role and specific company challenges[cite: 10].
Follow-up sequences
Draft polite, value-driven follow-up messages that reference previous attempts to connect[cite: 10].
Tone adjustment
Quickly rewrite an existing draft to sound more formal, concise or conversational[cite: 10].
Without Gemini
Sales representatives spend hours staring at a blank compose window, struggling to write unique hooks and value propositions for dozens of individual prospects every day[cite: 10].
With Gemini
You provide a few bullet points of context, and the AI instantly structures a professional draft, allowing you to focus purely on final personalization and strategy[cite: 10].

Building a structured workflow ensures that your AI-assisted outreach remains highly relevant and avoids sounding like an automated mass blast[cite: 10].

1
Research the prospect
Gather key information about the target's role, recent company news and specific business challenges manually[cite: 10].
2
Open the drafting tool
Open a new message in Gmail and launch the Gemini drafting assistant to begin your email[cite: 10].
3
Provide prompt context
Input your gathered research alongside your core value proposition and desired call to action[cite: 10].
4
Review and personalize
Refine the AI-generated draft to inject your unique voice and verify that the tone matches your sales strategy[cite: 10].
5
Send and track
Dispatch the finalized email through your standard Gmail workflow and prepare to monitor responses[cite: 10].

Note: Gemini tends to write longer paragraphs by default, so you will often need to explicitly ask it to keep the text short to accommodate modern email reading habits[cite: 10].

Using highly specific instructions is the only way to generate cold emails that read as authentic business correspondence rather than generic spam[cite: 10].

Prompt 1 — Initial cold email
Write a concise cold email to [Prospect Name], the [Job Title] at [Company]. Mention that I noticed they recently [Company News / Trigger Event]. Explain how our solution, [Product Name], helps solve [Specific Pain Point]. Include a soft call to action asking for a brief chat next week. Crucial instruction: Keep it under 100 words and do not invent any case studies, metrics or product features.[cite: 10]
Prompt 2 — Value-driven follow-up
Draft a short follow-up email to [Prospect Name] regarding my previous message about [Topic]. Provide one new piece of value, such as a link to our recent guide on [Subject]. Keep the tone polite and not pushy. Crucial instruction: Base the text only on these details. Do not invent pricing, discounts or false urgency.[cite: 10]
Before sending a Gemini-drafted cold email
Customization check: are all the placeholder names, companies and specific details correctly filled out?[cite: 10]
Brevity assessment: is the email brief enough to be easily read on a mobile device screen?[cite: 10]
Subject line alignment: does the subject line accurately reflect the actual content of the generated email?[cite: 10]
Tone verification: does the message sound like a human peer reaching out, rather than a robotic sales pitch?[cite: 10]
Link validation: have you successfully attached or linked the specific resources mentioned in the text?[cite: 10]
Important: AI cannot protect you from legal compliance issues regarding unsolicited commercial emails[cite: 10]. You must independently ensure your outreach adheres to strict anti-spam regulations, such as the CAN-SPAM Act in the USA and PECR in the UK, including providing a clear way for recipients to opt out[cite: 10].
