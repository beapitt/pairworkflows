Section 01
Why Gemini changes how HR teams work inside Google Workspace.

HR teams spend enormous amounts of time on repetitive writing — job descriptions, candidate emails, onboarding documents, policy updates, survey analysis. Most of this work already happens inside Google Workspace: Docs for policies, Sheets for people data, Gmail for candidate communication, Slides for training materials.

Gemini sits inside all of these tools natively. You don't copy data into an external AI — you open the Gemini side panel in the tool you're already using and ask it to help. This eliminates the biggest friction point: context switching.

The key advantage for HR: Gemini reads your actual Google Sheets data, your Docs content, and your Gmail threads. When you ask it to draft a candidate response, it can reference the actual conversation. When you ask it to analyse survey data, it reads the sheet directly.

Section 02
The workflow: from HR task to done — without leaving Workspace.

Candidate communication workflow

  1. Open the candidate's email thread in Gmail
  2. Click the Gemini icon to open the side panel
  3. Ask Gemini to draft a response — for example "Draft a professional but warm interview invitation for this candidate for next Tuesday at 2pm"
  4. Gemini reads the thread context and drafts the email
  5. Review, adjust the tone if needed, and send

Employee survey analysis workflow

  1. Open your survey results in Google Sheets
  2. Open the Gemini side panel
  3. Ask "Analyse the responses in column C. Identify the top 5 themes and flag any concerning patterns"
  4. Gemini reads the data directly and returns structured analysis
  5. Ask Gemini to draft a summary report in Docs for leadership
Without Gemini

Export survey data → paste into external AI → explain the columns → get analysis → copy into a Doc → reformat → draft the email summary separately. 5+ tools, 30+ minutes.

With Gemini

Open Sheets → ask Gemini to analyse → get results → ask Gemini to draft the summary in Docs → done. One ecosystem, under 10 minutes.

Section 03
Prompts that work: copy these directly into the Gemini side panel.

Job description drafting

Draft a job description for a [JOB TITLE] role in our [DEPARTMENT] team. Company context: [BRIEF COMPANY DESCRIPTION — size, industry, culture] Work model: [Remote / Hybrid / On-site] in [LOCATION] Reporting to: [MANAGER TITLE] Key responsibilities (from hiring manager notes): [PASTE MANAGER'S BULLET POINTS OR NOTES HERE] Requirements: - Must have: [LIST MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS] - Nice to have: [LIST PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS] Output structure: 1. About Us (2-3 sentences) 2. The Role (1 paragraph) 3. What You'll Do (5-7 bullets starting with action verbs) 4. What You Bring (split Required and Preferred) 5. Leave placeholders for [Salary Range] and [Benefits] Use inclusive language. Avoid jargon like "ninja" or "rockstar". Keep it professional but not stiff.

Interview feedback email

Based on this email thread, draft a professional response to the candidate. Context: [ACCEPTED FOR NEXT ROUND / REJECTED / ON HOLD] If rejected: be warm, thank them for their time, and encourage them to apply for future roles. Do not give specific feedback on their performance. If accepted: confirm the next interview date, provide logistics, and let them know what to prepare. Tone: professional, human, empathetic. Not corporate or template-sounding.

Employee survey analysis

Analyse the employee survey responses in this sheet. The responses are in column [COLUMN LETTER]. Each row is one employee response. Please: 1. Identify the top 5 recurring themes 2. Flag any responses that suggest urgent concerns (burnout, harassment, safety) 3. Summarise the overall sentiment as Positive, Mixed, or Negative 4. Suggest 3 concrete actions HR should consider based on the findings Format the output as a structured report suitable for sharing with the VP of People. Do not identify individual employees. Work only with the anonymised data in the sheet.

HR prompts checklist

Before using Gemini for HR work:
1. Anonymise all employee names and identifying information
2. Specify the audience — candidate, employee, manager, or leadership
3. Specify the tone — empathetic, formal, conversational
4. Include "Do not identify individual employees" for any survey or feedback analysis
5. Review every output for sensitivity before sending — AI can miss nuance in HR situations
