Step-by-step workflow

Set up your AI bookkeeping workflow — 4 steps
1

Match categories to your accounting software

The categories in the prompt are generic. Replace them with your exact account names from QuickBooks, Xero, or Wave — for example 6000 · Advertising or Consulting & Accounting. When Claude uses your real account names, you can import the output without remapping anything.

2

List your known personal vendors in the prompt

If you use a mixed account, add this line to the categorize prompt: These vendors are always personal — exclude from business categories: [Spotify, Netflix, local gym]. Claude assigns those automatically instead of flagging them every month.

3

Add a VAT or sales tax column if you need it

For UK businesses: add Apply 20% VAT to all business expenses unless the vendor is clearly exempt (salary, insurance, bank fees). For US businesses tracking sales tax on income: Flag any income line where sales tax may apply. Get rates confirmed by your accountant.

4

Run it monthly and build a year-to-date spreadsheet

Process one month at a time, paste each output into a running spreadsheet, and add a summary tab for year-to-date totals. By Q4 you have a full-year picture ready for your accountant. Same category names, same format, every month — consistency is the whole game.

Important: Claude categorizes based on the transaction description text you provide — it doesn't know your business context the way you do. Always review the output before importing into your accounting software, especially for high-value or unusual transactions.

Frequently asked questions

Can Claude replace my bookkeeper or accountant?
No. Claude handles the repetitive data work — categorizing, formatting, summarizing — so you spend less time on admin and your accountant spends less time cleaning up your records. Professional judgment, tax advice, and filing still need a real accountant.
Which accounting software does this work with?
Any of them. Claude outputs plain tables you can paste into Excel, Google Sheets, or import into QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, or FreeAgent. Tell Claude which category codes your software uses and it matches them — so you can import without remapping.
My bank exports in CSV. Can I paste that directly?
Yes. Copy the relevant columns — date, description, and amount are enough — and paste them into the prompt. Remove any summary rows or running balance columns first, as these confuse the categorization.
Can Claude produce a profit and loss statement?
Yes, using the Monthly P&L prompt tab above. Run the Categorize transactions prompt first — the category totals it produces feed directly into the P&L prompt. Claude formats and calculates the P&L clearly, including net margin percentage.
What if I mix personal and business spending on the same account?
Tell Claude in the prompt which vendors are always personal. It marks those automatically rather than flagging them every month. Ambiguous items still get flagged as "Uncategorized — Review" so nothing is silently mis-categorized.
Is this suitable for VAT or sales tax tracking?
Claude can add a VAT or tax column if you include the rate in your prompt. For mixed-rate or exempt transactions, flag them manually — getting tax rates wrong costs money to fix. Always confirm with your accountant before relying on Claude for tax classification.
