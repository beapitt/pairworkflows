Best Practices for Using AI in Operations

AI output in operations is only as good as the process description you give it. These practices keep the output accurate and safe to use.

Always do
  • Describe the process step by step before asking for a SOP
  • Include the audience — operator, manager, or auditor
  • Specify the format needed — checklist, narrative, or table
  • Always review AI output against your actual process before publishing
Never do
  • Use AI SOPs without review by the people who run the process
  • Rely on AI for safety-critical procedures without qualified expert review
  • Skip the format specification — vague prompts produce vague documents
  • Paste commercially sensitive supplier terms without anonymising

FAQ

Can Claude write SOPs for complex operational processes?
Yes — if you describe the process step by step. Claude structures the SOP with standard sections: purpose, scope, responsibilities, steps, and exceptions. The output needs review by the people who actually run the process before it goes live.
Can AI help with safety-critical procedures?
With caution. AI can draft structure and language, but safety-critical procedures require review by qualified safety professionals. Never publish an AI-drafted safety procedure without expert sign-off.
Can Claude structure incident reports consistently?
Yes. Paste your incident notes and Claude produces a structured report covering what happened, immediate actions, root cause, and recommendations. Useful for getting consistent outputs across different team members.
Can Gemini build inventory tracking models in Google Sheets?
Yes. One course shows how to build inventory tracking models with automated alerts — without needing to write complex formulas yourself.
Can AI help with supplier communication?
Yes. Claude can draft professional supplier emails, escalation notices, and coordination documents from your brief. Useful for maintaining a consistent tone across the team.
Can Claude help with KPI reporting narratives?
Yes. Give Claude your KPI data and the context behind the numbers. It drafts structured performance commentary suitable for operational reviews and board reporting.
Do these workflows scale for large operations teams?
Yes. The real value is standardisation — AI ensures every SOP, incident report, and KPI narrative follows the same structure, regardless of who writes it. Especially useful for distributed or multi-site teams.
Do these courses require a paid Claude or Gemini account?
No. All workflows work on the free tiers of both tools. Paid plans give higher usage limits but are not required to follow any course on this page.
