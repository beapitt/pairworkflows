3

Prompt for analysis and alerts

Paste the data into Gemini and ask it to identify low-stock items or summarise total category volumes — use Do not invent items every time.

Never automate the purchase order: Gemini may misinterpret a decimal point or a poorly formatted cell — a human procurement officer must validate the final numbers before submitting purchase orders to suppliers.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What can Gemini actually do with my inventory data?
Paste in your stock levels and minimum thresholds and Gemini can summarise current stock health, generate a reorder list for anything below its minimum, and highlight discrepancies between physical counts and system records. It works only from the data you paste in.
Does Gemini connect to my WMS or ERP system?
No. Gemini analyses only the data you provide in the prompt. It does not connect to your warehouse management system or ERP, cannot update stock levels automatically, and every report must be verified against your source data before you act on it.
Can Gemini factor in seasonal demand or upcoming promotions?
Not unless you tell it. Gemini cannot account for seasonal demand spikes or planned promotions unless you explicitly include that business context in your prompt — otherwise it only reorders against the historical minimum thresholds you gave it.
What should I check before acting on a Gemini inventory report?
Confirm every quantity and product code matches the raw export, check the reorder threshold logic is correct, verify units (pallets, boxes, units) weren't confused, and make sure no product lines were skipped. Never submit a purchase order on the summary alone.
Why do I need to tell Gemini not to invent inventory items?
Without an explicit instruction, a model can occasionally generate a plausible-looking reorder line for a product that isn't in your actual data. Adding "Do not invent items or change quantities" to the prompt keeps the output grounded strictly in what you pasted.
