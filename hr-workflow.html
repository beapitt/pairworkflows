What's a workflow — and how is it different from a prompt?

A prompt tells Claude what to do once. A workflow walks you through an entire HR process from start to finish: what context to gather first, what to give the AI, how to review the output, and what to do next. It's the difference between getting a single document and completing a real task.

These workflows are built for freelancers, independent HR consultants, and small business owners who handle HR without a dedicated team — people who need to move fast, produce professional output, and stay on the right side of employment law. No prior AI experience needed.
