Step-by-step workflow

Draft candidate communication with Gemini — 5 steps
1

Gather candidate status information

Collect the necessary context — the candidate's name, the role they applied for, and their current pipeline stage. Gemini can only work with what you give it.

2

Define the required tone and template

Decide whether the message should be warm and encouraging for next steps, or neutral and professional for a rejection, before you write the prompt.

3

Generate a draft with Gemini

Prompt Gemini with the candidate's details and the tone you need. Include do not invent instructions so the draft stays limited to confirmed facts.

4

Review and edit the draft

Read the generated text to confirm it sounds natural and that every placeholder and detail is correct before it goes anywhere near a candidate.

5

Send via your ATS or email client

Copy the finalised message into your applicant tracking system or email platform to send it to the candidate yourself.

No autopilot: Gemini does not have access to your applicant tracking system or candidate history, and it cannot send emails on your behalf. Every message is sent by your team through your normal tools — treat every draft as unsent until a recruiter has reviewed it.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What is Gemini's role in candidate communication?
Gemini drafts text only — it does not evaluate candidates, make hiring decisions, or send emails on your behalf. It generates a first-pass acknowledgement, status update, or rejection email based on the details you provide, and a recruiter reviews, edits, and sends every message.
Why should my prompt tell Gemini not to invent rejection reasons?
Without that instruction, Gemini can generate plausible-sounding but fabricated reasons or promises — a specific interview date, a reference to feedback that was never given. Adding "do not invent details not provided" keeps the draft limited to what you actually confirmed, which protects both the candidate and your company from miscommunication.
What should I check before sending a Gemini-drafted candidate email?
Run it through a quick checklist: tone matches your company voice, every date and job title is correct, no sensitive personal data has been mishandled, and every placeholder has been replaced with real details. A human recruiter should proofread every message before it reaches a candidate's inbox.
Can Gemini access my ATS or send emails automatically?
No. Gemini has no access to your applicant tracking system or candidate history, and it cannot send emails on its own. You paste in the relevant details, Gemini returns a draft, and your team copies the finalised text into your ATS or email client to send it.
How do I keep a rejection email neutral and respectful?
Give Gemini the candidate's name, the role, and the interview stage, then explicitly instruct it not to add specific reasons for the decision unless you want to include real, approved feedback. Thank the candidate for their time and state clearly that you're moving forward with other candidates — that combination keeps the tone professional without over-explaining.
