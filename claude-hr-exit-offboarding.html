Employee Offboarding Checklist Template with Claude AI

Every leaver triggers the same paperwork — a checklist, a handover template, a team message. Give Claude your standard offboarding steps and the basic facts, and it drafts all three, leaving the tone and the personal touch to HR and the manager.

Choose your situation — copy the prompt: 
Act as an HR assistant. I need an offboarding checklist for an employee leaving the company.

Employee role: [e.g. "Marketing Executive"]
Last working day: [Date]
Standard offboarding steps: [List your organisation's steps — e.g. "Revoke system access, collect laptop and ID badge, remove from email distribution lists, notify payroll, schedule exit interview"]

Turn this into a checklist with one item per line, grouped under headings such as "IT and Access", "Equipment", "Admin and Payroll", and "People". Do not add steps that are not in my list — if you think something is missing, note it separately as a suggestion.

Reuse this prompt with your standard steps every time someone leaves. Paste into Claude.

Act as an HR assistant. I need a short, neutral message announcing that a team member is leaving.

Employee name: [Name]
Role: [Role]
Last working day: [Date]
Handover plan: [Brief note on who is covering their work, if known]

Write a 3-4 sentence message that states the departure date, thanks the person for their contribution in general terms, and mentions the handover plan if provided. Do not speculate about or refer to the reason for leaving.

The manager should still personalise this draft before it goes to the team.

Act as an HR assistant. I need a handover template for a departing employee to fill in before their last working day.

Employee role: [Role]
Key areas of responsibility: [List the main projects, accounts or tasks this role covers]

Structure the template with sections for: Ongoing Projects (status and next steps), Key Contacts (internal and external), Recurring Tasks and Deadlines, Passwords and Access to Reassign, and Open Items Requiring Follow-up.

Crucial instruction: Produce a blank template with these headings only — do not invent project names, contacts or details that were not provided.

The departing employee fills this in — Claude only builds the structure.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Can Claude decide why an employee is leaving?
No. Claude doesn't know why someone is actually leaving. Any reference to the reason for departure gets added — or left out — by HR and the manager, based on what's appropriate and agreed with the employee.
What should a Claude-drafted team announcement leave out?
Any speculation about or reference to the reason for leaving. The draft should state the departure date, thank the person for their contribution in general terms, and mention the handover plan if provided — nothing more.
What if Claude thinks a step is missing from my offboarding checklist?
It should note the missing step separately as a suggestion rather than adding it directly to the checklist. Claude should not add steps that are not in your original list.
Who decides what gets said about a difficult departure?
HR and the manager, based on what's appropriate and agreed with the employee — never Claude and never the wider team through informal discussion.
Is it safe to paste employee details into Claude for offboarding documents?
Follow your organisation's data protection policy for any personal details included in drafts. Offboarding touches sensitive personal circumstances, so check what is appropriate to share before pasting it into any AI tool.
