How the prompt works

No invented steps

The AI analyses only the process steps you paste. It cannot add industry best practices or steps that don't exist in your actual workflow — keeping the output grounded in your reality.

Pain point mapping

Each bottleneck you describe is mapped to the specific step where it occurs — so you know exactly where to fix, not just that something is broken.

Structured SOP output

For SOP tasks, the output is a clean numbered sequence with owners and dependencies — ready to share with a team or use for onboarding without further editing.

Works for any scale

Whether you're a solo operator documenting your own process or mapping a multi-team workflow, the prompt adapts to the people and steps you describe.

Tip: Paste your process as a simple numbered list — even rough notes work. The AI reads the sequence clearly and produces better output from a plain list than from a paragraph description.
