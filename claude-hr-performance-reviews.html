Vague feedback vs specific feedback
Vague — unhelpful and risky
"Sarah has a good attitude and generally meets expectations. She could improve her communication skills going forward."
Specific — clear and defensible
"Sarah consistently met her Q1 targets and proactively flagged two project risks before they escalated. Development focus for H2: structuring written updates for senior stakeholders — her verbal communication is strong but written summaries need more concision."

How to get the best output from Claude
1

Be specific about what the employee actually did

Don't say "good performer" — say what they did, when, and what the impact was. Claude turns your notes into language. You provide the substance.

2

Use anonymous placeholders for personal data

Instead of a real name, use "the employee" or a generic role title. Never paste salary details, personal information or HR system data into Claude.

3

Read and edit the draft before using it

Claude produces a starting point, not a final document. Read every word. Change anything that doesn't accurately reflect your assessment of the employee.

4

Flag underperformance language for review

If the review addresses underperformance or is part of a formal process, have your HR or legal team review the language before it is shared or placed on record.

Frequently asked questions
Can Claude write performance reviews?
Yes. Give Claude the employee's role, key achievements, development areas and goals. It produces a structured, professional first draft. You must read, edit and approve every draft before it goes on record.
Is it safe to use Claude for HR documents?
Yes, with care. Do not upload employee names, personal data or salary details. Use Claude for drafting language only — the performance rating and all decisions remain with the manager. Have HR or legal review any underperformance or disciplinary language.
What information does Claude need?
Employee role, review period, overall rating, 3–5 specific achievements, 1–3 development areas with examples, and agreed goals. The more specific your input, the better the output.
Does this work with ChatGPT and Gemini too?
Yes. The prompts above work with Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Paste the prompt into whichever AI you use and fill in the bracketed placeholders with your real information.
