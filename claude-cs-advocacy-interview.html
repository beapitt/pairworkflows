Step-by-step workflow

Prep a case study interview with Claude — 5 steps
1

Pull the account history

Grab the original sales notes, onboarding timeline, and biggest support tickets from the last year. The more specific detail in here, the more specific the questions Claude can generate.

2

Define the case study angle

Figure out what story marketing actually wants to tell — a massive enterprise deployment, or a fast self-serve win? The angle shapes which parts of the history matter.

3

Paste the raw history and your angle into Claude

Drop the account notes and your specific angle right into the prompt window — don't summarise it yourself first, let Claude work from the source material.

4

Generate ten hyper-specific interview questions

Ask Claude to write questions based on the exact events in the notes — not generic templates. Instruct it explicitly not to ask ROI questions.

5

Review with marketing before the call

Take the list to your marketing team and cut any questions that don't fit the current campaign. Then conduct the interview and follow the interesting threads as they come up.

Important: You must obtain explicit written client consent before using their story, name, or company in any public marketing material. Never assume consent just because they agreed to jump on a quick call. Always get the final written case study approved by their legal team before you publish it.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What makes Claude effective for case study interview prep?
It reviews actual account history — onboarding notes, support tickets, quarterly review notes — to generate highly specific, customised questions instead of a generic template. Instead of asking how a client likes the software, it can point you to the exact week their servers crashed and your tool kept them online.
What is a clear limitation of using Claude for interviews?
Claude can't read body language or ask spontaneous follow-up questions during the live conversation. It prepares the question list beforehand, but you still have to actively listen and follow interesting threads once the client starts talking.
What must I secure before publishing the case study?
Explicit written client consent to use their story, name, and company. Never assume consent just because the client agreed to jump on a call. Always get the final written case study approved by their legal team before it's published.
What is the second step in the prep workflow after pulling account history?
Define the case study angle so you know what story you actually want to tell — a massive enterprise deployment, a fast self-serve win, or a specific implementation speed story. The angle shapes which questions Claude generates.
Can Claude tell me if my interview questions have gaps?
Yes. After Claude generates the question list, ask it to review the narrative arc the answers would produce and flag any missing story beats. This highlights where you may need a spontaneous follow-up question during the actual interview.
