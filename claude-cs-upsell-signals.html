Step-by-step workflow

Spot expansion signals with Claude — 5 steps
1

Collect 90 days of communications

Pull recent support tickets, email threads, and call notes for the account. The wider the window, the more patterns Claude can find.

2

Define your expansion criteria

List the premium features you can actually sell them so Claude has something concrete to map the signals against.

3

Paste it into Claude and identify signals

Ask Claude to scan for workflow bottlenecks and quote the exact sentence — not a vague summary — where the client mentions the pain point.

4

Build the upsell play

Turn the confirmed signal into a short, non-pushy talk track that frames the upgrade as a direct solution to their stated frustration.

5

Time the conversation carefully

Check the account's overall health and wait for the right moment — don't pitch in the middle of an active support crisis.

A signal is not permission to pitch: Spotting an expansion signal is not authorization to immediately pitch the client. Timing and relationship context are critical — premature upsell attempts damage trust.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What is Claude AI specifically looking for when scanning client communications for upsells?
Offhand feature requests and workflow bottlenecks that indicate a client is outgrowing their current plan — things like a complaint about a user seat limit or a slow export process.
What is a direct limitation of using Claude for this workflow?
Claude can't know the client's internal budget cycle or whether they are facing a company-wide spending freeze. You still have to uncover those economic realities yourself.
What is the golden rule after Claude spots a valid upsell signal?
Spotting a signal is not authorization to immediately pitch the client. Timing and relationship context are critical — jumping into an active support crisis to push an upgrade damages trust.
What is the first step in the workflow for spotting expansion signals?
Collect recent support tickets, email threads, and call notes for the account before you paste anything into Claude — the signal has to come from real evidence in the text.
How does Claude avoid flagging a false upsell signal?
When prompted correctly, Claude only flags a signal if there is actual evidence in the text and quotes the exact sentence where the client mentions the pain point, rather than guessing based on vague impressions.
