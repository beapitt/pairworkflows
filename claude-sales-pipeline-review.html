Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
How does Claude prioritize deals differently from a manual pipeline review?
Claude ranks deals by staleness, missing next steps, and stage-to-close ratio — not just by value. It applies the same criteria to every deal, so the review doesn't depend on which manager happens to be running it that week.
What columns does Claude need from my CRM export?
Deal name, stage, value, close date, last activity date, next step, and owner. These seven columns let Claude detect stalled deals, prioritize by impact, flag overdue deals, and group results by rep for coaching.
Should I anonymize pipeline data before pasting it into Claude?
Yes. Replace real company names with Company A, Company B, and so on, and replace contact names with Contact 1, Contact 2. Keep the numbers and dates real — those are what Claude actually needs to analyze.
What does Claude do when pipeline data is missing?
It does not invent information. If a deal is missing a close date or an owner, Claude flags it as "Incomplete data — needs rep input" instead of guessing, so the gap goes back to the rep rather than silently distorting the forecast.
How does Claude structure the forecast in a pipeline review?
It categorizes every deal into Commit, Best Case, or Upside based on the signals in your data, and shows the total value for each category — giving you a forecast built from concrete signals rather than gut feeling.
