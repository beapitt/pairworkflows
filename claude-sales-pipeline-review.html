Section 01

Your pipeline review is broken — here's why deals keep slipping.

Every sales team does pipeline reviews. Most do them badly. A manager opens a spreadsheet, scrolls through 40 deals, asks the rep for updates on each one, and writes notes that nobody reads again until the next review.

The result: deals that should have been flagged two weeks ago are still sitting in "Negotiation." Deals that need executive attention are buried under a list of smaller opportunities. And the forecast is a guess dressed up as a number.

The core problem: Pipeline reviews are subjective. Two managers looking at the same data will prioritize different deals. Claude makes the review structured, consistent, and data-driven — every time.

What Claude does differently

Prioritizes by risk

Claude ranks deals by staleness, missing next steps, and stage-to-close ratio — not just by value.

Flags hidden risks

Deals with no activity in 14+ days, deals stuck at the same stage, deals with no identified decision-maker.

Generates action items

For each flagged deal, Claude suggests the specific next step — not generic advice, but context-driven actions.

Structures the forecast

Claude categorizes deals into Commit, Best Case, and Upside based on the signals in your data — not gut feeling.

Section 02

The workflow: from raw pipeline data to a structured review in 15 minutes.

This workflow takes your CRM export or pipeline spreadsheet and turns it into a prioritized review with risk flags and action items. No custom tools needed — just Claude and your data.

Step 1: Prepare your data

Export your pipeline from your CRM or copy it from your spreadsheet. Claude works best with these columns:

ColumnWhat it containsWhy Claude needs it
Deal nameAccount or opportunity nameIdentifies the deal
StageCurrent pipeline stageDetects stalled deals
ValueDeal size in currencyPrioritizes by impact
Close dateExpected close dateFlags overdue deals
Last activityDate of last meaningful contactIdentifies cold deals
Next stepPlanned next actionFlags deals with no plan
OwnerRep assigned to the dealGroups by rep for coaching

Anonymize first: Replace real company names with Company A, Company B etc. Replace contact names with Contact 1, Contact 2. Keep the numbers and dates real — those are what Claude needs to analyze.

Step 2: Paste into Claude with the prompt

Copy your pipeline data and paste it directly after the prompt. Claude reads tables, comma-separated values, or plain lists. The prompt tells Claude exactly what to analyze and how to structure the output.

Step 3: Review and act

Claude returns a structured review. You scan the flagged deals, verify the risk assessment against your knowledge of each account, and decide on actions. The whole process takes 15 minutes instead of 2 hours.

Without Claude

You scroll through 40 deals in a spreadsheet, trying to remember which ones need attention. You spend 2 hours and still miss the deal that's about to slip because the last activity was 3 weeks ago.

With Claude

Claude scans every deal, flags the 6 that need immediate attention, explains why each one is at risk, and suggests the next step for each. You spend 15 minutes on decisions, not data gathering.

Section 03

The prompt: copy, paste your data, and get a structured pipeline review.

Pipeline review prompt

You are a senior sales operations analyst. I am going to paste my pipeline data below. Analyze it and produce a structured pipeline review. For each deal, assess: 1. Risk level (High / Medium / Low) based on: days since last activity, whether close date has passed, whether a next step is defined, and stage progression 2. Specific reason for the risk rating 3. Recommended next action Then organize your output as: DEALS NEEDING IMMEDIATE ATTENTION (High risk deals — list each with: deal name, value, risk reason, recommended action) DEALS ON TRACK (Low risk deals — brief summary only) FORECAST SUMMARY Categorize all deals into: - Commit (high confidence, close this period) - Best Case (likely but not certain) - Upside (possible but uncertain) Show total value for each category. FLAG any deal where: - Last activity is older than 14 days - Close date has passed - No next step is defined - Deal has been in the same stage for 30+ days Do not invent information. If data is missing for a deal, flag it as "Incomplete data — needs rep input." Here is my pipeline data: [PASTE YOUR PIPELINE DATA HERE]

What to expect from the output

Immediate attention

Deals flagged as high risk with specific reasons — "no activity for 21 days", "close date passed 2 weeks ago", "no next step defined."

On track

Deals with recent activity, defined next steps, and realistic close dates. Brief summary so you can skip quickly.

Forecast

Deals categorized into Commit, Best Case, Upside with total values. Ready to paste into your forecast report.

Data gaps

Deals where information is missing — "no close date", "no owner assigned." These need rep input before you can assess them.

Review checklist

After Claude returns the review:
1. Scan the high-risk deals first — do you agree with the flags?
2. Check the forecast categories — does Commit feel realistic?
3. Assign action items to reps for each flagged deal
4. Note any deals where Claude flagged missing data
5. Use the structured output in your team review meeting
