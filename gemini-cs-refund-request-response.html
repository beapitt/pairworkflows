Frequently asked questions

Does Gemini know my refund policy?
No. You must manually paste your policy text into the prompt so Gemini has the necessary context.
Can I automate refund processing?
No. Gemini acts as a drafting assistant. It has no authority to access your payment gateway or issue real financial transactions.
When should I involve legal?
Any request involving potential litigation, breach of contract, or significant financial disputes must be reviewed by legal counsel.
