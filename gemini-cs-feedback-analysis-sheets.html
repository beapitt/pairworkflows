How to Write an FAQ Document with Gemini AI in Google Docs

Paste your raw notes, email threads, and prospect questions into a Google Doc. Use Gemini to structure them into a clean FAQ document — with technical answers rewritten for non-technical buyers — in minutes instead of hours.

Choose your situation — copy the prompt: 
Act as a sales enablement manager. Take these rough notes and
customer questions:

[Paste your raw material here — email threads, call notes,
 Slack messages, product spec extracts]

Turn them into a structured FAQ document for enterprise buyers.

Requirements:
- Create clear Question and Answer pairs.
- Group related questions under logical section headings
  (e.g. "Onboarding", "Integrations", "Pricing & Contracts").
- Write each answer at a business level — not too technical,
  not too vague.
- Keep each answer to 2-4 sentences maximum.
- Base the answers strictly on the text I've provided.

Rules:
- Do NOT invent product features, pricing models, or timelines
  not in my notes.
- Do NOT include generic industry advice not specific to my product.
- If a question appears in my notes but the answer is unclear,
  flag it as "Answer needed — check with SME" rather than guessing.

Step-by-step workflow

Write an FAQ document with Gemini — 5 steps
1

Collect questions from real sources

Pull from email threads with prospects, discovery call transcripts, internal Slack channels where reps ask the same questions, and past proposals. These are the questions that actually stall deals — not hypothetical ones. Aim for 15–20 questions before running Gemini.

2

Source approved answers from SMEs — not from memory

For each question, pull the correct answer from product spec sheets, engineering documentation, or a confirmed response from a subject matter expert. Don't rely on what you think the answer is — Gemini will base its output on what you provide, so inaccurate input produces inaccurate FAQ answers.

3

Paste into Google Docs and run the Generate prompt

Open a new Google Doc, paste your raw questions and notes, and run the Generate FAQ draft prompt above. Gemini structures the content into Q&A pairs grouped under logical section headings. Any question without a clear answer gets flagged as "Answer needed — check with SME".

4

Run the Simplify prompt on technical answers

For any answer your engineering team provided, run the Simplify technical answers prompt. Specify the buyer persona — CFO, procurement manager, VP Ops — so Gemini uses the right business language. Apply manually after: Format → Paragraph Styles → Heading 2 for each section so the doc is searchable.

5

Verify every answer before publishing

Before sharing with the sales team: check every answer against current product capabilities, confirm no unauthorized warranties or pricing commitments were added, and remove any bracketed placeholders Gemini left in. One wrong answer in a FAQ used in 50 sales calls causes 50 problems.

Important: If you provide ambiguous or contradictory notes on pricing or legal terms, Gemini may produce an overly definitive commitment your company cannot honor. Always have legal or compliance review any FAQ section covering contracts, SLAs, or regulatory compliance before it goes to the sales team.

Frequently asked questions

What sources should I use to build an FAQ document?
The best sources are email threads with prospects, discovery call transcripts, internal Slack channels where reps ask repetitive questions, and product spec sheets. Past successful proposals are also valuable — they contain the questions that nearly derailed the deal and the answers that resolved them.
Can Gemini connect to my support ticketing system automatically?
No. Gemini in Google Docs generates content strictly from the raw data you provide in the document. It does not automatically integrate with Zendesk, Intercom, or other ticketing systems. You gather the questions manually and paste them in.
How do I make technical answers readable for non-technical buyers?
Use the Simplify technical answers prompt tab above. Paste the engineering team's answer and specify the buyer persona — CFO, procurement manager, VP Ops. Gemini rewrites it focusing on operational and financial benefits without inventing new capabilities.
Does Gemini add heading formatting automatically in Google Docs?
No. Gemini generates text but does not apply H1 or H2 heading styles. Apply heading formatting manually after the draft — use Format → Paragraph Styles → Heading 2 for each section so the document is searchable and navigable.
How often should I update the FAQ document?
Review and update quarterly at minimum. Add new questions whenever a rep encounters the same question from three or more prospects — that threshold signals it belongs in the FAQ. Use the Add new questions prompt tab above to integrate new Q&A pairs without disrupting the existing structure.
