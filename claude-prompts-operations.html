How Claude handles this prompt

Zero Invented Steps

The prompt uses a strict logic lock: Claude is instructed to analyze *only* the steps you provided in "Current Process". It cannot invent steps that don't exist in your company.

Pain-Point Mapping

Claude maps your listed Pain Points against the provided Process Steps to highlight exactly where the breakdown occurs, saving you from guessing.

Structured SOP

If creating an SOP, the output is formatted as a logical, numbered list of actions, owners, and dependencies, ready for team distribution.

Professional Tone

The output is formal, objective, and action-oriented — optimized for internal documentation and stakeholder review.

Tip: If your workflow is complex, paste it as a simple list. Claude works best when it can clearly see the sequence of events (e.g., Step 1 -> Step 2).
