Paste data from Excel or Sheets

After copying the prompt, open your AI and paste it. Then add your line-item figures directly below — copy-paste from Excel or Google Sheets works perfectly.

No invented figures

If data is missing, the AI flags exactly what is needed rather than inventing numbers. The prompt is designed to be safe for real financial workflows.

Audience-aware tone

The prompt adjusts tone based on your selected audience — concise and strategic for the Board or CFO, more detailed for internal teams or personal use.

Actionable output

Every report includes root-cause hypotheses for flagged variances and corrective actions — not just numbers. Designed to be shared without further editing.
