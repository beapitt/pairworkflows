Summarize Bug Reports and Feature Requests with Gemini AI

Paste a frustrated client's raw email or call transcript into Gemini and get back a clean, engineering-ready brief — Observed Behavior, Expected Behavior, Steps to Reproduce, and Business Impact, with the emotion stripped out.

Choose your situation — copy the prompt: 
Review this client email regarding a platform issue: [Insert Email]. Create a
structured bug report using these exact headings: Observed Behavior, Expected
Behavior, Steps to Reproduce, and Business Impact.

Crucial instruction: Base the details strictly on the provided text. Strip
out all emotional language and do not guess the root technical cause of the
issue.

Paste into Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT.

Analyze this transcript of a client feature request: [Insert Transcript].
Format this into a standard product management User Story (As a... I want
to... So that...). Follow it with a brief summary of why this feature would
help them commercially.

Crucial instruction: Do not invent product capabilities or assume how the
engineering team should design the user interface.

Use this when the client is asking for something new rather than reporting something broken.

Here is a structured bug report I drafted: [Insert Bug Report]. Review only
the Business Impact section and strengthen it using details from this
original client context: [Insert Original Email/Transcript].

Crucial instruction: Only use figures, timelines, or commercial stakes that
are explicitly stated in the source text. Do not estimate revenue at risk or
invent a client tier if it was not mentioned.

A clear business impact line is what gets a bug prioritized over the dozens of other tickets in the backlog.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What is a fundamental limitation of Gemini when processing a bug report?
Gemini strictly summarizes the text you provide — it cannot autonomously test the reported bug in your environment or verify whether a requested feature already exists on the internal product roadmap.
Why should I carefully review an AI-generated bug report before escalating it?
Gemini may misinterpret a complex client configuration error as an actual software defect. A human review step catches this before an invalid bug gets escalated to engineering and wastes their time.
What security step should I take before pasting client feedback into Gemini?
Scrub the text of any passwords, API keys, or personally identifiable information a frustrated client may have inadvertently pasted into their support email before it ever reaches the prompt.
How should I format a feature request for a product team?
As a structured user story — As a [user type], I want to [action], so that [outcome] — followed by a short note on the commercial rationale, rather than a raw emotional transcript of the client's complaint.
Should I use the same structure for a bug report and a feature request?
No. Bug reports need Observed Behavior, Expected Behavior, Steps to Reproduce, and Business Impact. Feature requests are better served by a user story format plus commercial rationale — mixing the two formats confuses the receiving team.
