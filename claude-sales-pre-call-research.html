Step-by-step workflow

Build a pre-call stakeholder brief with Claude — 5 steps
1

Check the calendar invite — list every attendee

Note every name on the invite, including people you don't recognize. An unfamiliar name from Legal or Finance is often a signal that the deal has escalated to a new stage — that person is worth researching even if they're listed as optional.

2

Gather text from three sources per person

For each attendee: copy their LinkedIn bio, the relevant section of the company's website (team page, about us, or recent news), and any notes from previous calls or emails. Paste all of it into one document — Claude handles large blocks of mixed text well.

3

Run the Stakeholder Map prompt

Paste all your gathered text into Claude with the Stakeholder Map prompt above. Specify the exact headings — Core Champions, Hidden Blockers, Influence Mapping — so you get a structured brief rather than a generic summary paragraph.

4

Add your own observations manually

Claude only knows what's written. It can't see that the IT Lead's tone changed when you mentioned pricing on the last call, or that the CFO arrived late and left early. Layer your own gut instincts and personal observations on top of the brief before you open your slide deck.

5

Run the pre-call checklist

Before dialling in: verify that Claude stayed grounded in your text (no invented relationships), check that risk flags are based on real data points, and confirm you have a tailored talking point for each key decision-maker — not one generic pitch for the room.

Important: AI-generated briefs organize clues — they are not verified facts. If Claude flags a stakeholder as a risk based on a past role, cross-check that detail yourself before shifting your entire sales strategy around it.

Frequently asked questions

What is stakeholder mapping in sales?
Stakeholder mapping identifies everyone involved in a buying decision, their influence level, and potential blockers — before your sales call. It helps you focus on the people who actually control the decision rather than the people with the most impressive titles.
Can Claude see real-time information about a prospect?
No. Claude only knows what you paste into the prompt. You gather the text — LinkedIn bios, company pages, call transcripts — and paste it in. Claude organizes it into a structured brief. It cannot browse LinkedIn or access your CRM directly.
How accurate are Claude's risk flags in a stakeholder brief?
Risk flags are based only on the text you provide — they are useful starting points, not verified facts. If Claude flags a stakeholder based on their background, cross-check that detail yourself before shifting your strategy around it.
How long does it take to build a stakeholder map with Claude?
Gathering source text takes 10–15 minutes. The Claude prompt takes under 2 minutes. Adding your own observations takes 5 minutes. Total: around 20 minutes versus an hour of manual tab-switching and note-taking.
Can I use this for existing accounts, not just new prospects?
Yes — often more effectively. For existing accounts you have call transcripts, email threads, and internal notes that give Claude richer context than public data alone. Use the Renewal / expansion prompt tab above for these calls.
