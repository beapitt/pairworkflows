Step-by-step workflow

Build a tailored adoption plan with Claude — 5 steps
1

Capture the client's profile and constraints

Write down the client's team size, technical maturity, and the specific business goal they bought the product to achieve. This is the input Claude needs to reshape your standard sequence.

2

Paste your standard checklist plus the profile into Claude

Don't make Claude guess what your product does. Feed it the real onboarding checklist you already use, alongside the client's profile, so it has a concrete framework to reshape.

3

Generate the tailored adoption journey

Ask Claude to connect every training milestone to the client's specific goal — not just list generic tasks. A startup and an enterprise account should get visibly different plans.

4

Draft the executive summary

Take the tailored plan and generate a one-page summary focused on day 30 and day 60 business outcomes for the client's project sponsor — not a list of configuration steps.

5

Validate the plan against the contract and the client

Check the plan against actual contractual deliverables, then walk through it with the customer on the kickoff call to confirm the dates match their real availability.

Contract check is mandatory: Never present an AI-generated plan that promises timelines, integrations, or features not explicitly covered in the signed contract. Always review against actual deliverables before sending.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What is the main benefit of using Claude AI for onboarding plans?
Claude reshapes your standard onboarding checklist into a custom adoption journey based on the client's technical maturity, team size, and stated business goals, instead of sending every client the same generic 30-day PDF.
What is a direct limitation of using Claude to build an adoption plan?
Claude can't sync directly with your internal project management software to assign tasks. It generates the plan and the milestones, but you still have to manually build the final project board and assign owners yourself.
What must I do before sending an AI-generated onboarding plan to a client?
Always review the plan against the actual contractual obligations and agreed deliverables in the signed agreement. Never present a document that promises timelines, integrations, or features that are not explicitly covered in the contract.
Why should I validate the plan with the customer during a kickoff call?
Claude works from the profile and constraints you give it, but it can't know the client's actual internal availability. A kickoff call lets you adjust the suggested dates based on their real bandwidth and get explicit buy-in on the timeline.
How does Claude adjust the plan for startups versus enterprise clients?
For a fast-moving startup, Claude compresses the plan to get one core workflow live in 48 hours and defers advanced configuration. For an enterprise account, it recommends piloting with a single department first and using early wins to build a business case for company-wide rollout.
