How Claude handles this prompt

Strict Word Limits

Sales emails fail when they are too long. This prompt forces Claude to adhere to strict word counts (e.g., under 120 words for cold emails) to maximize readability on mobile devices.

Banning AI "Sales Speak"

The prompt explicitly bans standard AI intros like "I hope this email finds you well" and pushy sales jargon, forcing a tone that sounds like a peer-to-peer business conversation.

Problem-First Approach

Instead of pitching your product immediately, the prompt instructs Claude to lead with the prospect's pain point, proving you understand their industry before offering the solution.

Fact Guardrails

Claude is instructed to rely solely on the Value Proposition and Pain Points you provided. It will not invent case studies, fake metrics, or unverified claims to win the pitch.

Tip: The "Call to Action (CTA)" should be low-friction. Instead of asking for a 30-minute demo immediately, try testing softer CTAs like asking a specific question related to their pain point.
