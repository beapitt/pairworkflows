Frequently asked questions

Are all courses really free?
Most courses on PairWorkflows are completely free. A small number of advanced courses are marked as Premium — these will be available for a one-time fee. Free courses will always remain free.
Do I need a Claude or Gemini subscription to follow the courses?
No subscription is required to take the courses. However, to practise what you learn, a free or paid Claude or Gemini account is recommended. Some courses reference features available only on paid plans.
How long does each course take?
Each mini-course is designed to take 5 minutes or less. They are built for busy professionals who need practical answers fast — not hour-long lectures.
Which AI tools do the courses cover?
PairWorkflows covers two tools: Claude AI (by Anthropic) and Gemini AI (by Google), including its integration with Google Workspace. Both tools are widely used in corporate environments across the UK and USA.
Are the courses suitable for non-technical professionals?
Absolutely. PairWorkflows is designed for managers, analysts, marketers, HR professionals, and anyone who uses AI tools at work — no coding or technical background required.
