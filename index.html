Frequently asked questions

Are all courses really free?
Most guides on PairWorkflows are completely free. A small number of advanced courses are marked as Premium — these will be available for a one-time fee. Free guides will always remain free.
Do I need a Claude or Gemini subscription to follow the courses?
No subscription is required to follow the guides. However, to practise what you learn, a free or paid Claude or Gemini account is recommended. Some courses reference features available only on paid plans.
How long does each course take?
Each mini-guide is designed to take 5 minutes or less. They are built for busy professionals who need practical answers fast — not hour-long lectures.
Which AI tools do the courses cover?
PairWorkflows covers two tools: Claude AI (by Anthropic) and Gemini AI (by Google), including its integration with Google Workspace.
Are the courses suitable for non-technical professionals?
Absolutely. PairWorkflows is designed for managers, analysts, marketers, HR professionals, and anyone who uses AI tools at work — no coding or technical background required.
