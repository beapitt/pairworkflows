Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What does Claude need from me before it can help with SEO content optimization?
The existing article text and your target keywords. Claude works on the text you provide — it does not do autonomous keyword research or access Google Search Console.
Why should I instruct Claude not to invent keyword search volumes?
To prevent Claude from adding unverified data that could mislead your SEO strategy. Base every audit strictly on the provided text, not on assumed search volumes.
What SEO data limitation does Claude have?
It does not know your current rankings, traffic data or competitor keyword strategies. That data must come from your own SEO tools before you start the optimization.
What must I do after Claude rewrites my SEO content elements?
Validate the changes against your actual search data and current SEO best practices. Claude's knowledge has a training cutoff and cannot reflect the latest algorithm changes.
Will an SEO rewrite from Claude still sound like my brand?
Only if you check it. Ask Claude to keep the brand voice consistent with the existing text, then read the rewrite yourself to confirm it still sounds like your brand, not generic SEO copy.
