Step-by-step workflow

Set up the Microsoft 365 connector — 5 steps
1

Open Settings and go to Connectors

In Claude, click your profile icon, go to Settings, then select Connectors. You'll see available integrations including Microsoft 365.

2

Authorise your Microsoft 365 account

Click Connect on the Microsoft 365 connector. You'll be redirected to sign in with your work Microsoft account and grant Claude read-only access.

3

Select only what Claude needs

Choose specific SharePoint sites, Outlook folders, or Teams channels rather than granting access to everything. Less is more — targeted access means sharper, faster responses and less exposure.

4

Use it in conversation

Once connected, tell Claude what to look at by name: Check the Q2 report in the Finance SharePoint and summarise the key risks. Claude finds it, reads it, and responds.

5

Confirm your plan supports it

The connector requires a Claude Pro or Team plan, and your organisation may need to enable third-party connectors in workspace settings. Check with IT if the option doesn't appear.

Not a sync tool: Claude doesn't copy or cache your files. It reads them on demand, so you always get the current version — and nothing sits in Claude's memory after the conversation ends.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What happens to an uploaded file every time you send a message?
It is re-processed in full and consumes usage on every single message in the conversation, even after it's no longer relevant to what you're discussing. The bigger the file, the more it costs on every turn.
What does the Microsoft 365 connector do when Claude needs a file?
It reads the file on demand from the live source — SharePoint, Outlook, or Teams — without storing or caching it. You always get the current version, and nothing sits in Claude's memory after the conversation ends.
What is the best practice when setting up the connector?
Connect only the specific SharePoint sites, Outlook folders, or Teams channels Claude actually needs — not your entire Microsoft 365 environment. Targeted access gives sharper results and keeps your data exposure to a minimum.
What is the key advantage of the connector over manual uploads?
You always get the current live version of a file, not a downloaded copy that may already be out of date. There is also no usage drain from a large file sitting in the conversation, and nothing is stored after the chat ends.
What plan do I need for the Microsoft 365 connector?
The connector requires a Claude Pro or Team plan. Your organisation may also need to enable third-party connectors in workspace settings — check with your IT team if the option doesn't appear.
