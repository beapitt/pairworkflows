How the prompt works

No blank page

The prompt gives the AI everything it needs to produce a complete first draft from your inputs — no generic filler, no invented content.

No invented content

The AI uses only what you provide. If key details are missing, it flags them rather than making things up — keeping the output accurate and usable.

Inclusive by design

The prompt instructs the AI to avoid gendered language, corporate jargon, and exclusionary terms — following modern inclusive hiring standards.

Ready to use

Output is structured and ready to paste into your ATS, HRIS, or document — minimal editing required.

Tip: The more specific your role objective and requirements, the better the output. Paste real manager notes or bullet points from your intake form — the AI turns rough inputs into polished language without inventing details.
