Section 01

Building equitable territory and quota plans takes weeks. Gemini generates formulas and summaries from your Sheets data.

Balancing sales territories and setting equitable quotas based on historical performance, account potential and market coverage is a complex and time-intensive process. Sales operations leaders often spend weeks manually building formulas and cleaning large spreadsheets to segment accounts and distribute targets fairly.

Gemini in Google Sheets serves as a powerful analytical assistant. By feeding it your account data and using natural language prompts, you can quickly generate complex formulas, segment accounts into tiers and summarize revenue by region or territory. This significantly reduces manual effort and lets sales leaders focus on strategic decisions rather than spreadsheet mechanics.

Key insight: Gemini works only with the static data present in your current spreadsheet — it cannot pull live information from your CRM or automatically map physical geographical boundaries.

A standard account tiering model uses three revenue bands:

Tier 1
$500K+
High-value strategic accounts — priority coverage
Tier 2
$100K – $499K
Mid-market accounts — standard coverage
Tier 3
Below $100K
SMB or growth accounts — lean coverage

Note: Gemini may misinterpret complex proprietary scoring models unless you clearly define the exact mathematical criteria in your prompt.

Precise, constrained prompts ensure the AI generates formulas that match your exact column layout and business rules rather than generic examples.

Prompt 1 — Account tiering formula
Act as a Sales Operations Manager. Write a Google Sheets formula that evaluates the 'Historical Revenue' in column C. If the revenue is above $500,000, label it 'Tier 1'. If it is between $100,000 and $499,999, label it 'Tier 2'. Otherwise, label it 'Tier 3'. Crucial instruction: Output only the exact formula for cell D2. Do not add extra tiers or external scoring logic.
Prompt 2 — Regional revenue summary
Analyze the sales data in columns A through F. Generate a summary table showing total historical revenue grouped by 'State' and 'Account Tier'. Crucial instruction: Base everything strictly on the data in the sheet. Do not estimate missing values or exclude accounts.
Before finalizing a Gemini-assisted territory and quota plan
Calculation accuracy: did you test the AI-generated formulas on multiple sample rows?
Equity check: is the quota distribution mathematically balanced across territories?
Filter verification: did the AI correctly handle dormant or excluded accounts per your instructions?
Data completeness: are all geographical fields properly represented in the summaries?
Placeholder removal: have all generic references been updated to match your exact column layout?
Important — human judgment required
AI-generated territory and quota plans are based solely on historical data. They do not consider important human factors such as a representative's tenure, industry expertise or existing client relationships. Sales leaders must always apply final qualitative judgment before rollout.
