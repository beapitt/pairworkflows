Frequently asked questions

Can Gemini directly upload macros to my CRM?
No. You must manually copy the text into your system's macro library.
Why must I manually adjust the placeholders?
Gemini uses generic bracketed text. You must replace these with your CRM's exact syntax rules so automation doesn't break.
