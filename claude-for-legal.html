Best Practices for Using AI in Legal

AI in a legal context requires extra discipline. These practices protect confidentiality, maintain accuracy, and keep every output within appropriate professional boundaries.

Always do
  • Specify governing law and jurisdiction in every prompt
  • Include the contract type and your standard position
  • Anonymise party names and confidential commercial terms
  • Always have qualified legal counsel review every AI output
Never do
  • Treat AI output as legal advice — it never is
  • Rely on AI for final sign-off on any legal document
  • Paste genuinely confidential deal terms without anonymising
  • Skip the qualified review on anything that creates legal obligations

How to anonymise before pasting: replace party names with Party A / Party B, replace deal values with representative figures, replace confidential commercial terms with generic descriptions. The AI produces the same quality output — without the confidentiality risk.

FAQ

Can Claude review contracts?
Yes — Claude can surface key clauses, flag unusual terms, and identify deviations from your standard position. It cannot provide legal advice. Every output needs qualified legal review before any decision is made.
Is AI output legally reliable?
No AI output is legally reliable without qualified human review. These courses speed up drafting and analysis — they do not replace legal judgment.
Can Claude draft NDAs and service agreements?
Claude can generate structured first drafts from your brief. The draft needs review by qualified legal counsel before use. The drafting course covers how to brief correctly to get a useful first draft.
Can Gemini compare two versions of a contract?
Yes. One course covers how to use Gemini to surface differences between document versions in Google Docs — useful for redline review and amendment tracking.
Is it safe to paste contract data into Claude or Gemini?
Anonymise first — replace party names, deal values, and confidential commercial terms with placeholders. For sensitive matters, use your organisation's enterprise AI account with appropriate data controls.
Can AI help with regulatory compliance monitoring?
Yes — for summarising and structuring. One course covers how to use Gemini to monitor regulatory updates and draft structured summaries of changes and implications.
Do these workflows work for in-house legal teams as well as law firms?
Yes. The workflows are designed for in-house counsel and legal operations teams dealing with high document volumes. They work equally well for small legal teams without specialist support for every document type.
Do these courses require a paid Claude or Gemini account?
No. All workflows work on the free tiers of both tools. Paid plans give higher usage limits but are not required to follow any course on this page.
