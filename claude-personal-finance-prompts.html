- Personal / clothing / misc: [amount]
- [Add any others]

Savings goals:

- [Any other savings goal — e.g. holiday, house deposit]

1. Build a categorized monthly budget table with columns: Category | Monthly Amount | % of Income
2. Calculate total expenses, total savings, and what's left unallocated

4. Tell me how long it will take to reach my emergency fund target at my current savings rate
5. Suggest one or two adjustments if my budget is too tight
