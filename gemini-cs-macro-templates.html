Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Can Gemini directly upload macros to my CRM?
No. Gemini generates the text for your templates, but it does not automatically upload or configure them within your CRM or helpdesk software. You must manually copy the text into your system's macro library.
Why must I manually adjust the placeholders?
Gemini naturally uses generic bracketed text for missing information (like [First Name]). You must manually replace these with your system's exact syntax rules (e.g., {{ticket.requester.first_name}}) so your automated merge fields do not break in production.
Is it risky to rely entirely on macros?
Yes. While macros ensure accuracy and speed, sending templates without a touch of contextual personalization can degrade the client experience. Always review the macro and add a human element before hitting send.
How do I ensure the tone matches my company?
The best approach is to feed Gemini a few examples of perfectly toned past emails from your top performers. This constrains the AI to mimic your actual corporate voice rather than generating generic, robotic responses.
Can I create variations for different client tiers?
Absolutely. Use the "Adjust tone" prompt to instruct Gemini to rewrite a single baseline macro into a formal "Enterprise" version and a slightly more conversational "Mid-market" version, maintaining the same factual resolution.
