How Claude handles this prompt

Zero Hallucinations

The prompt explicitly forbids Claude from inventing product features, discounts, or offers that you didn't provide in the inputs. You maintain total control over the claims.

Channel Optimized

Whether you choose LinkedIn, Email, or Landing Page, the prompt instructs Claude to format the output natively (e.g., using subject lines for emails, or scannable bullet points for web).

No Marketing Clichés

Claude is instructed to avoid tired jargon like "game-changer," "revolutionary," or "synergy." The result is modern, punchy, and persuasive copy.

Conversion Focused

By defining a strict Target Audience and Goal, Claude structures the narrative to lead naturally to your specified Call to Action, maximizing click-through rates.

Tip: Be as specific as possible in the "Key Message / Value Proposition" field. Don't just say "It saves time." Say "It saves 10 hours a week by automating data entry." Claude relies entirely on this field to persuade the reader.
