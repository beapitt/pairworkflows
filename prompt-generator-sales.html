How the prompt works

Strict word limits

Sales emails fail when they're too long. The prompt enforces strict word counts per asset type — under 120 words for cold emails — to maximise readability on mobile.

No AI sales speak

The prompt bans openers like "I hope this finds you well" and pushy jargon, forcing a tone that sounds like a peer-to-peer business conversation.

Problem-first approach

The prompt leads with the prospect's pain point before mentioning the product — showing you understand their situation before offering the solution.

No invented claims

The AI uses only the value proposition and pain points you provided. It will not invent case studies, fake metrics, or unverified claims.

Tip: Keep your CTA low-friction. Instead of asking for a 30-minute demo immediately, try a softer ask like a specific question related to their pain point — it gets more replies.
