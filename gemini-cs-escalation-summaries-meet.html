Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
Can Gemini verify the factual accuracy of a client's claims during an escalation call?
No. Gemini strictly summarizes the spoken transcript — it cannot cross-reference the client's timeline against your internal system logs or verify whether their technical claims are accurate.
Why might I need to manually review a transcript of a heated escalation call?
Gemini may struggle to accurately attribute dialogue when multiple people talk over each other, which happens frequently during heated, emotional calls.
What should I do if an escalation call includes mentions of financial penalties?
Have your legal department manually review any commitments regarding financial compensation or contractual liability — an AI summary is a business communication, not a legally binding record.
Why filter out emotional language when prompting Gemini for an escalation brief?
To produce a sterile, objective brief focused on operational facts so leadership and engineering can act on it immediately, instead of reading a transcript of raw frustration.
What should an escalation brief always include?
Three sections: the core client complaint in their own words, the stated business impact, and the required internal actions with named owners and deadlines.
