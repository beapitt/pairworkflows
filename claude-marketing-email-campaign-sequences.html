Claude AI for Email Campaign Sequences

Writing a welcome flow or nurture series one email at a time loses the thread between messages. Give Claude your campaign goal, audience and key messages once, and get a full connected sequence back — subject lines, body copy and calls to action, ready to refine.

Choose your situation — copy the prompt:

Campaign goal: [Insert Goal]
Brand tone: [Insert Tone, e.g. professional, warm, direct]
Key messages to include: [Insert Key Messages]

Crucial instruction: Base all copy on the details provided. Do not invent product features, pricing, statistics or claims not included in this brief.

2

Plan the number of emails and timing

3

Write a brief and ask Claude to draft the sequence

5

Set up the sequence in your sending tool, check rendering across devices and send a test before activating.

Verify compliance before sending: Email marketing regulations — including consent requirements, unsubscribe rules and data handling — vary by country and change over time. Always verify that your sequence complies with the rules applicable to your audience, such as GDPR in the UK and EU or CAN-SPAM in the USA.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions
What does Claude need to draft an email sequence?
A brief covering the goal, audience, tone and key messages. Claude drafts from the brief you provide — it does not access your email platform or subscriber list.
What limitation does Claude have when drafting email sequences?
It does not know your past email performance or what has worked for your audience. Subject line decisions, send timing and segmentation should be guided by your own email performance data, not by Claude.
Why must I check email compliance before activating a Claude-drafted sequence?
Because email regulations vary by country and Claude cannot verify compliance on your behalf. Always confirm your sequence complies with the rules applicable to your audience, such as GDPR or CAN-SPAM.
What should guide subject line and send timing decisions?
Your own email performance data and audience behaviour — not Claude's general suggestions. Claude doesn't know what has historically worked for your specific list.
Can I rewrite just one email in a sequence without redoing the whole thing?
Yes. Give Claude the current email, the specific goal of that email, and the tone of the rest of the sequence, then instruct it not to add product claims or offers beyond what's already there.
