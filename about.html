About

AI should do the work.
You should stay in control.

That's the idea behind PairWorkflows. The name comes from the word pair — not because the AI does everything, but because the best results come from working together: you bring the judgment, the context, and the final call. Claude brings the speed, the formula logic, and the heavy lifting.

Passive delegation — "AI, do it all" — produces generic output nobody trusts. Active collaboration — where you guide, review, and correct — produces work you can actually use.

PairWorkflows exists to make that collaboration concrete: here's the exact prompt, here's what it does, here's what Claude gives back. Copy it, use it, adjust it.

What's on the site

Right now PairWorkflows focuses on one territory: Excel with Claude AI. It's a narrow focus by design — the SERP for generic AI advice is crowded with big names. The gap is specific, practical, written content for Excel users who want to use Claude without watching a 20-minute YouTube tutorial.

Excel Workflows

Step-by-step guides for formulas, errors, finance models and data cleaning — with the exact prompt to paste into Claude.

Prompt Builders

Interactive tools that generate a ready-to-use Claude prompt from your inputs. Fill in the details, copy, paste, done.

Website Prompts

Multi-step prompt builders for creating restaurant and small business websites with Claude or ChatGPT.

Always free

No paywalls, no signup required. Every workflow and tool on this site is free to use.

Who it's for

Anyone who opens Excel regularly and wants to spend less time wrestling with formulas and errors. Finance analysts, accountants, operations managers, HR teams, small business owners — anyone who uses spreadsheets as part of their actual job, not as a hobby.

The workflows assume you know roughly what you want to achieve but aren't sure how to get Excel and Claude to work together to get there.

How it's built

PairWorkflows is a static site — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted on Vercel. No database, no backend, no tracking beyond basic analytics. It loads fast and works on any device.

Every workflow is written and tested by a human. Claude helps with drafting and iteration, but the prompts are verified against real Excel problems before they go live.
