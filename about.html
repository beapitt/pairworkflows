What PairWorkflows is

PairWorkflows is an independent, free resource. There is no company behind it, no team, no investors and no advertising. It is built by one person who spends real hours every day testing Claude and Gemini workflows on the kinds of tasks corporate professionals actually face — finance reporting, HR documentation, contract review, marketing briefs, operations processes.

Every course that gets published has been tested first. If a workflow does not work as described, it does not go live.
