Best Practices for Using AI in HR

AI in HR requires extra care. These practices protect employees, keep you compliant, and ensure the output is actually useful.

Always do
  • Include role, seniority level, and context
  • Specify the exact output format needed
  • Anonymise employee names and sensitive HR data
  • Always apply human judgment before any HR decision
Never do
  • Use AI output for final disciplinary or legal decisions without review
  • Paste identifiable personal data into any AI tool
  • Rely on AI alone for sensitive conversations
  • Skip human review on anything that affects employment

How to anonymise before pasting: replace employee names with Employee A, use role titles instead of names, remove any identifiable details. The AI produces the same quality output — without the data risk.

FAQ

Can Claude draft performance reviews without sounding generic?
Yes — if you give it the employee's role, key achievements, and areas to develop. The more specific your input, the better the output. You always refine before sharing it with the employee.
Is it safe to paste employee data into Claude or Gemini?
Not identifiable data. Anonymise first — Employee A, Role Title, etc. The courses show you exactly how to structure prompts safely without exposing personal data.
Can AI help with CV screening without introducing bias?
AI can summarise and structure information against your criteria. It should not score or rank candidates autonomously. Use it as a support tool, then apply your own judgment. The course covers how to do this correctly.
Can Claude write HR policies that are legally compliant?
Claude can draft clear policy language quickly. It cannot provide legal advice. Every policy must be reviewed by your legal or compliance team before publication.
Can Gemini analyse employee survey results?
Yes. Paste anonymised survey responses and ask Gemini to identify themes, sentiment, and risks. One course covers the exact workflow in Google Sheets.
Can AI help with redundancy or disciplinary communications?
With caution. AI can help structure clear language. Any communication affecting employment must be reviewed by HR leadership and legal before sending.
Do these workflows work for small HR teams?
Yes. They are especially valuable for lean teams — helping one person handle the workload of several without specialist support for every function.
Do these courses require a paid Claude or Gemini account?
No. All workflows work on the free tiers of both tools. Paid plans give higher usage limits but are not required to follow any course on this page.
