How the prompt works

No invented claims

The prompt explicitly forbids the AI from inventing product features, statistics, or offers you didn't provide. You stay in full control of every claim.

Channel-optimised format

Whether you choose LinkedIn, email, or landing page, the prompt instructs the AI to format the output natively — subject lines for emails, scannable structure for web.

No marketing clichés

The AI is instructed to avoid overused phrases like "game-changer", "revolutionary", or "unlock your potential". The result is direct, modern copy.

CTA-focused structure

The copy is structured to lead naturally toward your specified call to action — no filler paragraphs after the CTA, no weak closings.

Tip: The more specific your value proposition, the better the output. Don't just say "it saves time" — say "it saves 10 hours a week by automating receipt tracking." The AI relies entirely on what you provide to write persuasive copy.
