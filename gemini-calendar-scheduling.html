Section 01

Stop losing your valuable work hours to calendar admin.

Managing a corporate schedule often feels like a full-time job in itself. Between endless email chains to find a mutual meeting time and the time spent manually preparing for each session, your actual productivity takes a massive hit.

Gemini in Google Calendar is designed to reclaim that lost time. The goal is to move beyond basic scheduling and automate the entire workflow that happens before and after your professional commitments.

Constant email back-and-forth
Searching for common availability between colleagues creates a frustrating ping-pong of messages that fragments your focus and disrupts your daily flow.
Lack of meeting preparation
Entering a meeting without a clear agenda or context turns efficient discussions into unproductive sessions that run over time.
Lost follow-up actions
Without an automated system, the critical decisions and tasks agreed upon during a meeting often slip through the cracks once the session ends.

Practical tip: Use Gemini's time suggestions to finalise scheduling in under 30 seconds, skipping the need for back-and-forth emails entirely.

Gemini is not just a reminder tool — it is an intelligent engine that analyses your commitments and communications to maximise every minute spent in meetings.

Natural language scheduling
Type a simple phrase like "Team sync next Tuesday at 10 AM" and Gemini handles the invite creation, duration, and attendee list instantly.
Optimal time analysis
It scans your team's real-time availability to suggest the best possible time slots that minimise disruption for everyone involved.
Automated agenda generation
Gemini generates structured meeting agendas based on recent email context, ensuring everyone knows the meeting focus beforehand.
Pre-meeting briefings
Before your session, it provides a concise summary of past interactions or documents relevant to the meeting participants.
Follow-up automation
Post-meeting, it prepares a summary of key decisions and action items, ensuring nothing is forgotten after the call concludes.

Practical tip: Use the briefing function five minutes before every major meeting to walk into the room with a significant information advantage.

Implementing Gemini into your daily workflow takes minimal effort but dramatically changes how your entire department manages its time.

1
Enable in Admin Console
Ensure your IT department has fully enabled Gemini for Google Workspace, as these advanced features remain hidden without system-wide authorisation.
2
Configure your preferences
Navigate to your Calendar settings and define your specific working hours and meeting preferences so Gemini knows when you are truly available.
3
Connect relevant documents
Link the necessary Drive folders to your calendar, allowing Gemini to pull the right information for creating accurate and detailed agendas.
4
Create reusable templates
Save your agendas for recurring meetings like 1-on-1s or weekly briefings as templates so Gemini can deploy them automatically for future events.

Important: Always review sensitive data or confidential documents before letting Gemini summarise them, as your Drive sharing permissions determine exactly what the AI can read.
