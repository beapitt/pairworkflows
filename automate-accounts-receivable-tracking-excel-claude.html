Automate Accounts Receivable Tracking in Excel with Claude AI

Accounts Receivable (AR) is the lifeblood of cash flow. Manually calculating days past due and sorting invoices into aging buckets is a waste of finance hours, and relying on stale data destroys your ability to collect effectively. You need to know exactly which clients are in the 90+ day bucket today, not last week.

Claude takes your raw invoice exports, builds dynamic formulas to calculate outstanding days against the current date, and automatically categorizes every balance into standardized aging buckets (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 90+). Instead of manual sorting, you get an automated tracking report that updates the moment you open the file.

01

Standardize your payment terms

If your system exports terms as "Net 30" in one row and simply "30" in another, formula logic breaks. Ensure your terms column uses standard numeric days (e.g., 30, 45, 60) so Claude can easily calculate due dates mathematically.

02

Include partial payments

If you don't track the 'Amount Paid' against the invoice, you will end up chasing customers who have already settled their accounts. Ask Claude to calculate the Remaining Balance (Billed - Paid) and only flag balances > 0.

03

Define your exact aging buckets

Don't let Claude guess. Tell it explicitly: Current (Not due), 1-30 Days, 31-60 Days, 61-90 Days, and 90+ Days. This ensures the output matches your internal reporting standards.

04

Require dynamic dates

Never let Claude hardcode a date. The prompt must explicitly instruct the use of Excel's TODAY() function so that days outstanding increment automatically over time without manual intervention.

Common mistake: Calculating 'Days Overdue' based on the Invoice Date instead of the Due Date. If you give a client 60-day terms, they are not past due on day 30. Ensure Claude's logic subtracts the Due Date from TODAY(), not the Invoice Date.
Pro tip: Once the table is built, ask Claude for the instructions to build a PivotTable summarizing the total outstanding balances by Customer and Aging Bucket. This gives you a high-level executive view for your weekly finance meeting.

The Claude Prompt for an Automated AR Schedule

This prompt works with Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT.

Prompt — paste into AI
ROLE You are a senior FP&A analyst and Collections Manager building an automated Accounts Receivable (AR) aging schedule in Excel. OBJECTIVE Take my raw invoice data, write the Excel formulas to calculate remaining balances, determine due dates, calculate days past due dynamically, and assign each invoice to an aging bucket with a collection status. DATA [Paste your raw invoice data here: Invoice #, Customer, Date Issued, Terms (Days), Amount Billed, Amount Paid] TASK Create a new comprehensive table with the following columns and logic: 1. All original data columns. 2. Remaining Balance = Amount Billed - Amount Paid 3. Due Date = Date Issued + Terms (Days) 4. Days Overdue = Use the TODAY() function. If Remaining Balance is 0, return 0. Otherwise, MAX(0, TODAY() - Due Date). 5. Aging Bucket = Use an IFS statement based on Days Overdue: - 0 days = "Current" - 1-30 days = "1-30 Days" - 31-60 days = "31-60 Days" - 61-90 days = "61-90 Days" - > 90 days = "90+ Days" - If Remaining Balance is 0, return "Closed". 6. Status = Flag "Closed" as Green. Flag "Current" as Green. Flag "1-60 Days" as Amber (Follow Up). Flag "> 60 Days" as Red (High Risk). CONSTRAINTS - Formulas MUST use TODAY() to ensure the sheet is dynamic. - Write the exact Excel formula string for the first row of data (assuming data starts in row 2) so I can copy/paste it into my spreadsheet. - Output a tab-separated table of the calculated results. OUTPUT FORMAT - The calculated tab-separated table. - A clear list of the exact Excel formulas required for the new calculated columns.

Tracking AR is Just the Beginning

Knowing that an invoice is 90 days past due is vital, but the impact of that delay hits your bank account. If major clients are shifting into the Amber and Red buckets, your available cash will dip. The AR report feeds directly into your broader financial operations.

If overdue invoices are starting to threaten payroll, you need to plug these figures into a cash flow dashboard to model the short-term impact. Furthermore, consistently high Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) should be monitored at the executive level through a structured financial KPI dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Claude automate AR tracking?
Claude writes the formulas to calculate days outstanding dynamically, assigns invoices to aging buckets, and applies conditional formatting rules to flag high-risk overdue accounts.
Can Claude handle partial payments?
Yes. If you provide an 'Amount Paid' column, Claude will generate a 'Remaining Balance' calculation and apply aging logic exclusively to the unpaid portion of the invoice.
What is an aging schedule?
An aging schedule categorizes outstanding accounts receivable according to the length of time an invoice has been overdue (e.g., 30, 60, 90 days), helping finance teams prioritize collections.
Do I need to manually update the current date?
No. The prompt strictly instructs Claude to use the Excel TODAY() formula. Your aging report will automatically calculate days past due relative to the actual day you open the workbook.
How do I handle different payment terms?
Feed a numeric 'Payment Terms' column (e.g., 15, 30, 60) into the prompt. Claude calculates the specific Due Date for each invoice by adding those terms to the invoice issuance date.
